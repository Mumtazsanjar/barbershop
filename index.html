Our Story

Craft, Character
and Community.

The Gentlemen's Club was founded on a simple conviction: that a great barbershop is more than a place to get a haircut. It's a place where craftsmanship is taken seriously, where every client is treated with respect, and where the quality of work speaks louder than any marketing.

Over more than a decade, we've built a team that takes pride in the fundamentals — clean lines, precise blends, and a thorough understanding of each client's face and style. We invest in the best tools, the best products, and the continuous education of every barber on our floor.

Walk in with whatever you've got. Leave with a cut that fits you perfectly.

  • Precision in every cut
  • Premium products only
  • Continuous barber training
  • Respect for your time
  • Classic barbering tradition
  • Welcoming to every client
